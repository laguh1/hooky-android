# Hooky — User Guide

Hooky is a personal crochet and knitting companion app. It helps you track your works in progress, manage your yarn and needle collection, build a stitch library, and keep on top of the business side of your craft.

## Overview

The app is organised into four main sections, accessible from the bottom navigation bar:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | At-a-glance summary of your activity |
| **Pieces** | Your crochet and knit items |
| **Yarns** | Your yarn inventory |
| **Stitches** | Your stitch technique library |

Needles, Search, the Price Calculator, Reports, and Settings are accessible from within the app (via the top bar or menus depending on the screen).

---

## Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a quick overview of your crafting activity:

- **In progress** — pieces you are currently working on, with shortcuts to jump straight to them
- **Total pieces** — how many pieces you have logged overall
- **Hours worked** — total time recorded across all your pieces
- **Revenue** — total amount from pieces you have sold

Tap any in-progress piece card to go directly to that piece.

## Pieces

Pieces are the heart of Hooky. A piece is any item you are making or have made — a shawl, a blanket, a toy, a bag, anything.

### Creating a Piece

Tap the **+** button on the Pieces screen. Fill in as much or as little as you like:

- **Name** — what you call this project
- **Type** — shawl, blanket, toy, etc.
- **Status** — In progress, Finished, or Ready to sell/gift
- **Destination** — For sale, Sold, For gift, Gifted, For self, In use
- **Dimensions** — width and height (in whatever unit you prefer)
- **Date started / finished**
- **Work hours** — total hours and minutes spent
- **Yarns used / Stitches used / Needle used** — pick from your existing inventory
- **Material cost** — what you spent on materials for this piece
- **Price / Sold price** — if it is for sale or has been sold
- **Gift recipient** — if it is going to someone specific
- **Notes** — any extra information

Photos can be added via the camera button — you can take a new photo or pick one from your gallery. See [Photos & Photo Editor](#photos--photo-editor) below.

### Row Counter

On any piece's detail screen you will find the **Row Counter**. It tracks how many rows you have completed.

- Tap **+** or **−** to increment or decrement one row at a time
- Long-press **+** to jump forward by a larger number
- Tap the row number itself to open a dialog and set an exact count or define a target row goal
- Use the **microphone** button for hands-free counting — the app listens for a number spoken aloud and adds that many rows (handy when your hands are full of yarn)

When a target row is set, a progress bar shows how far through the piece you are.

### Work Timer

The **Work Timer** lets you track time on a per-session basis without having to remember to fill in hours manually.

- **Start** — begins a new timed session
- **Pause / Resume** — pause without losing the elapsed time
- **Stop** — saves the session to the piece's work log

The timer survives the app being closed or the phone restarting — when you reopen the piece, it picks up where it left off.

**Pace estimation:** Once you have logged at least one session that includes both time and rows, the app calculates and displays your average pace as minutes per 10 rows. This updates automatically as you log more sessions.

### Extra Counters

In addition to the main row counter, you can add any number of **custom counters** to a piece — useful for tracking pattern repeats, increases, decreases, or anything else you need to count independently.

- Tap **Add counter** and give it a name (and optionally a target)
- Each counter has its own + / − buttons and a reset option
- Long-press a counter to edit or delete it

### Photos & Photo Editor

Each piece can have multiple photos. Tap the photo area to open the gallery, then:

- **Camera icon** — take a new photo
- **Gallery icon** — pick an existing photo from your device
- **Edit icon** (pencil) on any photo — open the built-in photo editor

The **Photo Editor** opens full-screen with the photo and three controls at the bottom:

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Rotate left | Rotates the photo 90° counter-clockwise |
| Flip | Mirrors the photo horizontally |
| Rotate right | Rotates the photo 90° clockwise |

Tap **Save** to keep your changes, or **×** to discard them and go back.

### Price Suggestion

On a piece's detail screen, tap **Suggest price →** to open the price suggestion tool. It calculates a recommended selling price based on:

- Material cost you have entered
- Hours logged (valued at a standard labour rate)
- A profit margin

You can adjust the inputs in the dialog. Tap **Apply** to copy the suggested price into the piece's price field.

### Share Card

On a piece's detail screen, tap the **Share** button to generate a clean share card image with the piece's photo, name, and key details. The Android share sheet opens so you can send it to Instagram, WhatsApp, or wherever you like.

### Archiving a Piece

To remove a piece from your active list without permanently deleting it, tap the **Archive** option (accessible via the top-right menu on the detail screen). You will be asked to choose a reason. Archived pieces are preserved in full — they are never deleted.

## Yarns

The Yarns section is your material inventory. Keep track of what you have, how much of it, and all its care details.

### Adding a Yarn

Tap **+** on the Yarns screen. Fields include:

- **Name / Brand**
- **Color name and color code** (e.g. the manufacturer's code)
- **Color** — detected automatically from a photo (see below), or enter manually
- **Material** — acrylic, wool, cotton, etc.
- **Weight category** — lace, fingering, DK, worsted, bulky, etc.
- **Ball weight** (grams) and **length** (metres)
- **Price per ball** and **quantity owned**
- **Where you bought it**
- **Recommended hook sizes** and **needle sizes** — can be entered manually or scanned from the label
- **Care instructions** — machine wash, hand wash, dry clean, bleach, tumble dry, wash temperature, notes

### Yarn Label Scanner

Tap **Scan Label** on the yarn form to use your camera (or pick a photo from your gallery) to scan a yarn label. The app uses on-device OCR to read the text from the label and automatically fills in fields like brand, weight, material, and care symbols.

After scanning, a confirmation dialog shows you what was detected — you can review and adjust before applying it to the form.

If a label has two sides, tap **Scan another side** to keep reading.

### Hook & Needle Size Scanner

On the yarn form, next to the recommended hook/needle size fields, there is a scan button. Point your camera at the size marking printed on a needle or hook and the app reads the size for you.

### Color Detection

When you add or pick a photo for a yarn, the app automatically detects the dominant color and fills in the color field. You can always change it manually.

### Care Instructions

The care instructions section uses standard symbols and a wash temperature dropdown (30°C, 40°C, 60°C, 95°C, or Do not wash). These are displayed on the yarn's detail screen as a quick-reference badge row.

## Stitches

The Stitches section is your personal technique library.

### Adding a Stitch

Tap **+** to add a stitch. Fields include:

- **Name** — the standard English name
- **Aliases** — other names you may know it by
- **Category** — basic, textured, lace, colorwork, or specialty
- **Difficulty** — beginner, intermediate, or advanced
- **Tutorial links** — URLs to written instructions or YouTube videos
- **Notes**
- **Photos or stitch chart images** — upload diagrams or charts from your gallery

### Stitch Chart Upload

On the stitch form or detail screen, use the **Upload chart** button to add a stitch diagram image from your device gallery. Charts are displayed alongside the stitch details for easy reference while you work.

---

## Needles

The Needles section tracks your hook and needle collection.

### Adding a Needle

Tap **+** to add a needle or hook. Fields include:

- **Type** — crochet hook, knitting needle, double-pointed, etc.
- **Size** — in mm (or the equivalent letter/number size)
- **Material** — aluminium, bamboo, plastic, etc.
- **Brand**
- **Notes**
- **Photos**

Like yarns, you can scan the size marking on a needle using the camera scan button — the app reads the size and fills in the field.

---

## Search

Tap the **search icon** (top bar, or swipe-to-search on the list screens) to search across your entire library at once. Results from Pieces, Yarns, Stitches, and Needles appear grouped together. Tap any result to go directly to that item.

---

## Price Calculator

Access the **Price Calculator** from the top bar on the Pieces screen (calculator icon). It helps you work out a fair selling price for any project:

1. Enter the **material cost**
2. Enter **hours worked**
3. Set a **labour rate** (default €8/hr — adjustable)
4. Choose a **complexity factor** and **profit margin**

The calculator shows the suggested price and a cost breakdown. You can use this standalone or use the **Suggest price** shortcut directly on a piece's detail screen.

---

## Reports

Access **Reports** from the top bar on the Dashboard or Pieces screen. Reports give you a summary of your crafting output and finances:

- Pieces completed by period
- Hours worked by period
- Revenue and profit from sold pieces
- Cost breakdown (material vs. labour)

## Settings

Tap the **gear icon** (top bar) to open Settings.

### Appearance

Choose between **Light**, **Dark**, or **System default** (follows your device's display setting).

### Language

Choose between **English**, **Spanish**, or **Portuguese (Brazil)**, or leave it on **System default** to follow your device language.

### Currency

Select your preferred currency — **EUR (€)**, **USD ($)**, **GBP (£)**, or **BRL (R$)**. All price fields across the app display in the selected currency.

### Backup

Your data is stored locally on your device. To protect it:

- **Export backup** — saves a backup file to your device (you can then copy it to cloud storage, email it to yourself, etc.)
- **Import backup** — restores from a previously exported file. **Warning: this replaces all current data.**

It is a good habit to export a backup whenever you make significant changes.

---

*Hooky stores all data locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Your projects, yarns, and stitches are yours.*

## Rules used in every caption (keep them when editing)
- Say **Hooky App**, never just "Hooky".
- The first line works without the video: a question or problem a crocheter or knitter recognises.
- Say "carreiras", not "fileiras". Use gender-neutral PT: no "vendedora" or "crocheteira".
- Pro episodes (1, 8) say **Recurso Pro, com 7 dias grátis para testar** / **A Pro feature, with a 7-day free trial**. Episode 4 says more counters come with Pro.
- Every caption closes with the "always improving" line:
  - IG: link in bio
  - FB: comments or `/feedback`
  - YT: the `/feedback` link
- Links:
  - Instagram: `hookyapp.netlify.app/ig`
  - Facebook: `hookyapp.netlify.app/fb`
  - YouTube: `hookyapp.netlify.app/yt` and `hookyapp.netlify.app/feedback`
- Mark the series with **Novidades do Hooky App · N/8** / **What's new in Hooky App · N/8**, the same words as the video's opening screen.
- **YouTube:**
  - Title: under 70 characters, ending in `#shorts`.
  - Description: the same 4 links every time.
  - Tags: paste them into the Tags field, not the description.

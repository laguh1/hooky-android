# Hooky — Video Tutorial Script

**Target length:** 3–5 minutes  
**Tone:** Warm, practical, direct — like a fellow crafter showing you something useful  
**Format:** Screen recording of the app + voice narration  
**Tip:** Record in one room with no background noise. Read each block slowly — you can always speed up in editing, but you can't slow down a rushed recording.

---

## INTRO — (~20 sec)

> If you do crochet or knitting — and especially if you sell your work — you know the feeling. Scattered notes, mystery yarn skeins, no idea how long a piece actually took. Hooky fixes that.
>
> It's a free app that tracks your projects, your yarn stash, your stitches and your needles — all in one place, offline, no account needed. Let me show you how it works.

*[Show app icon opening / splash screen]*

---

## DASHBOARD — (~20 sec)

> When you open Hooky, you land on the Dashboard. It gives you a quick view of what you're working on right now and what you've recently finished.
>
> These cards are your active pieces — tap any one to open it.

*[Tap an in-progress piece card]*

---

## PIECES — Adding a new piece (~45 sec)

> Let's add a new project. Tap the + button.

*[Tap FAB on Pieces tab]*

> Give it a name. Then choose the type — shawl, blanket, hat, whatever you're making. Set the status to "In Progress" and choose the destination: is this for yourself, for a gift, or for sale?

*[Fill in the form — name, type, status, destination]*

> You can add photos right from here — take one with the camera or pick from your gallery.

*[Tap camera or gallery button, show photo added]*

> If you know your dimensions, add them. And you can set when you started. Don't worry about filling everything — you can always edit later.
>
> Hit Save.

*[Tap Save — piece appears in list]*

---

## ROW COUNTER & TIMER — (~35 sec)

> Open that piece and scroll down. Here's the row counter. Tap + to count up, − to go back if you make a mistake. You can also tap the number to type a specific value — useful if you're continuing from a previous session.

*[Demonstrate tapping +/−, then tap number to edit]*

> Next to the counter is the work timer. Tap Start when you sit down to work. It runs in the background even if you close the app.

*[Tap Start on the timer]*

> When you're done for the day, tap Stop. The time logs automatically to the piece. This is how the price calculator knows how many hours you've put in.

*[Tap Stop — time logged]*

---

## COST & PROFIT — (~30 sec)

> Scroll further and you'll find the price calculator. Enter your material cost — what you paid for the yarn — and your hourly rate. Hooky calculates the total cost automatically using the logged work time.
>
> Then set your sale price. You'll see the profit and margin right there.

*[Enter values — show profit calculation update live]*

> No more guessing what to charge. You know exactly what your time and materials are worth.

---

## YARN STASH — (~30 sec)

> Now let's look at Yarns. This is your stash — every yarn you own, searchable and organised.
>
> Tap + to add one. Hooky can scan your yarn label with the camera to fill in the details automatically.

*[Tap camera scan button — show scan result dialog]*

> It reads the weight, fibre content, needle size, even the colour. You just confirm and it's done.

*[Accept scan result]*

> You can also log care instructions, purchase price, and where you bought it. So when you're in a shop wondering if you already have this yarn at home — you'll know.

---

## STITCHES — (~25 sec)

> The Stitches tab is your personal technique library. Add any stitch you use regularly.

*[Navigate to Stitches, tap +]*

> Give it a name, paste in a tutorial link — YouTube, a blog, anywhere — and rate the difficulty. You can also upload a chart photo or PDF.

*[Show a stitch detail with chart photo]*

> When you're working on something tricky and need to remember how a stitch goes, it's all here.

---

## NEEDLES — (~20 sec)

> Needles works the same way. Add your hooks and knitting needles, with size in mm, type, and brand.

*[Navigate to Needles, show list]*

> You can scan a needle to read the size automatically. Filter by hooks or knitting needles to find what you need fast.

>
> Download it free — the link is in the description.

*[Show Play Store listing or app icon + QR code]*

## RECORDING TIPS

- **Screen recording:** use your phone's built-in screen recorder (swipe down → Screen Record). Mirror to your desktop via scrcpy or QuickTime if you want to record from your computer.
- **Keep taps deliberate and slow** — viewers need time to see what you're pressing.
- **Pause half a second** before and after each tap — gives you edit points.
- **B-roll idea:** a few seconds of hands doing actual crochet before the intro — sets the context instantly.
- **Music:** soft lo-fi or no music at all — craft audiences respond better to clarity than production.
- **Subtitles:** auto-generate in YouTube and clean them up — essential for non-native speakers.
